# Keyword search with BM25

BM25 is a classic ranking function that scores documents by how often query terms appear, weighted by how rare each term is across the collection. Rare terms count for more through inverse document frequency. Term frequency saturates, so repeating a word ten times does not make a document ten times better. Length normalisation stops long documents from winning just because they contain more words. BM25 is strong when the exact words matter, such as names, codes and technical terms, and it needs no model or training.
